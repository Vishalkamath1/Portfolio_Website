# Standard GPA Declined Categories & Non-Preferred Risks

When assessing Group Personal Accident (GPA) proposals, the following occupational profiles and industries are typically declined or flagged as non-preferred across the Indian insurance market due to extreme hazard exposure:

-   **Defense & Armed Forces:** Navy, Military, Air Force, or other defense-related profiles.
-   **Mining:** Surface and underground mining of any sort.
-   **Legal:** Advocates and Bar Council members.
-   **High-Hazard Heavy Groups:** Any industry where more than 50% of the total group size is engaged in Class 4 (extreme hazard) activities.
-   **Offshore Operations:** Rigs, platforms, and related offshore activities.
-   **Height & Electrical:** Working on active electrical transmission lines or employed to work at heights exceeding 10 meters from the ground.
-   **Transportation:** Commercial drivers.
-   **Social/Religious:** Religious or social sector groups (e.g., mass pilgrims).
-   **Class 4 Individuals:** Any specific individual mapped to Risk Class 4 occupational hazards.

# Standard GPA General Exclusions

Unless explicitly waived or endorsed with additional premium, policies generally do not provide benefits for losses, expenses, or death resulting in whole or in part from:

-   **Pre-existing Conditions:** Any disability or injury arising from a pre-existing medical condition.
-   **Self-Treatment/Family Treatment:** Treatment by a physician who is either the Insured Person themselves or a close family member.
-   **Intentional Harm:** Suicide, attempted suicide, or intentional self-injury.
-   **Disease/Infection vectors:** Mosquito bites or similar vectors (unless strictly classified under accidental scopes in specific market variations).
-   **Intoxicants:** Being under the influence of drugs, alcohol, or other non-prescribed intoxicants.
-   **Criminal Acts:** Participation in an actual or attempted felony, riot, crime, or misdemeanor.
-   **Aviation Hazard:** Serving as a member of the crew on any aircraft or Scheduled Aircraft.
-   **Nuclear/Radiation:** Radioactive, toxic, explosive, or other dangerous properties of any explosive nuclear equipment, as well as ionising radiation.
-   **Unjustified Hospitalization:** Confinement in a Hospital which is not Medically Necessary.
-   **Maternity:** Any loss resulting from or prolonged by childbirth or pregnancy.
-   **War:** War, acts of war, invasion, or acts of foreign enemies.
-   **Bone Density/Degenerative:** Any loss caused by osteoporosis.
-   **Adventure Sports:** Participation in extreme or adventure sports.
